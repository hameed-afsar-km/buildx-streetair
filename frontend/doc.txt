1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CONCEPTUAL FOUNDATION
================================================================================

STREETAIR is an environmental intelligence SaaS platform designed to transition
from a high-fidelity startup hackathon demonstration into an enterprise-grade
B2B2G environmental monitoring and decision-support system.

It bridges low-cost IoT edge sensing (ESP32 micro-stations), neural anomaly
detection, automated regulatory compliance (CPCB/SPCB), and citizen science.

TARGET SECTORS (B2B2G):
- Higher Educational Campuses & Universities (e.g. Crescent Institute)
- Smart City & Municipal Authorities (e.g. CMDA, Greater Chennai Corporation)
- Industrial Manufacturing & Construction Corridors (e.g. Maraimalai Nagar, GST)
- Environmental Consultants & Compliance Auditors
- Public Citizens and Commuters

REGULATORY STANDARDS COMPLIANCE:
- Indian National Air Quality Index (NAQI) 6-Category Standard:
  * Good (0–50): Minimal health impact.
  * Moderate / Satisfactory (51–100): Minor breathing discomfort to sensitive people.
  * Poor / Moderately Polluted (101–200): Breathing discomfort to people with asthma.
  * Unhealthy / Poor (201–300): Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure.
  * Very Unhealthy (301–400): Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure.
  * Severe / Hazardous (401–500): Affects healthy people, severely impacts those with disease.
- Monitored Pollutants: PM2.5 (µg/m³), PM10 (µg/m³), NO2 (ppb), SO2 (ppb),
  CO (ppm/mg/m³), O3 (ppb), VOC, Temperature (°C), Relative Humidity (%).


================================================================================
2. CORE WORKFLOW & DATA PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

The platform operates across six interconnected operational phases:

  [1. MONITOR]  --> Continuous telemetry ingestion from physical hardware or
                    demonstration scenario engine via FastAPI and WebSockets.
  [2. DETECT]   --> Threshold checks and isolation forest models identify
                    micro-sensor spikes and spatial plume clusters.
  [3. INVESTIGATE]-> Spatio-temporal triangulation correlating sensor readings
                    with wind vectors and crowd-sourced citizen observations.
  [4. ANALYSE]  --> Neural source apportionment (Pollution Detective) attributes
                    likely causes (vehicular, dust, industrial, biomass).
  [5. PREDICT]  --> LSTM and Prophet models generate 24h to 72h rolling forecasts
                    with confidence intervals and diurnal variance.
  [6. RESPOND]  --> Automated emergency dispatches, webhook alerts (Slack/Teams),
                    and one-click CPCB Schedule VI environmental audit reports.

- Condition: Heavy diesel bus and truck congestion along the GST Road corridor.

KEY 3: CONSTRUCTION DUST
- AQI: 185 (Unhealthy) | PM2.5: 42 µg/m³ | PM10: 240 µg/m³ | NO2: 18 ppb
- Condition: Elevated coarse particulates from flyover civil engineering works.

KEY 4: INDUSTRIAL EMISSIONS
- AQI: 245 (Very Unhealthy) | PM2.5: 112 µg/m³ | SO2: 68 ppb | NO2: 92 ppb
- Condition: Chemical plume transport from Maraimalai Nagar industrial belt.

KEY 5: WASTE BURNING
- AQI: 320 (Hazardous) | PM2.5: 188 µg/m³ | CO: 4.8 mg/m³ | VOC: 420 ppb

6. DATA INTEGRITY, LABELS & SCIENTIFIC TRANSPARENCY
================================================================================

To guarantee scientific and legal credibility, STREETAIR strictly adheres to
data attribution principles:

1. SOURCE DISCLOSURE BADGES:
   Every telemetry reading displays an immutable DataSourceBadge:
   - "HARDWARE": Raw measurement from physical ESP32 optical / chemical sensor.
   - "SIMULATED": Generated by scenario emulation engine for demonstration.
   - "DEMONSTRATION": Synthetic calibration packet for UI evaluation.

2. STATUS BADGES:
   - "LIVE": Packet received within the last 30 seconds.
   - "STALE": No packet received for > 60 seconds; connection under recovery.
   - "HISTORICAL": Stored database rollup point.
   - "FORECAST": Future projection generated by LSTM / Prophet models.

3. SPATIAL INTERPOLATION DISCLAIMERS:
   All spatial heatmaps and route navigation calculations carry mandatory notices:
   "Estimated spatial interpolation based on discrete sensor nodes. Atmospheric
   mixing and micro-topography may cause localized variances."

7. TECHNOLOGY STACK & DESIGN SYSTEM SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

FRONTEND STACK:
- Framework: Next.js 14.2.35 (React 18, App Router)
- Language: TypeScript 5 (Strict Mode enabled, zero any types)
- Styling: Tailwind CSS 3.4 with custom environmental semantic tokens
- Data Fetching: TanStack React Query v5 + Native WebSocket abstraction
- Geospatial Maps: Leaflet 1.9 + OpenStreetMap (SSR-safe dynamic import)
- Visualizations: Recharts 2.12 (AreaChart, BarChart, LineChart)
- UI Icons: Lucide React
- Authentication: Firebase Authentication Client SDK
- Build Engine: PostCSS, Webpack, Autoprefixer

COLOR SYSTEM TOKENS:
- Good Air Quality: Emerald (#10B981 / bg-emerald-500)
- Satisfactory / Moderate: Amber (#F59E0B / bg-amber-500)
- Poor / Moderately Polluted: Orange (#F97316 / bg-orange-500)
- Unhealthy: Red (#EF4444 / bg-red-500)
- Very Unhealthy: Purple (#8B5CF6 / bg-purple-600)
- Severe / Hazardous: Maroon / Rose (#881337 / #E11D48)
